Ceremonial Scripts puts the ritual alphabet on your keys.

This is an unofficial fan project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Sleep Token or their label.

THE KEYBOARD

A system keyboard whose keycaps wear the runes. You type the way you always have, and the text that arrives is plain a to z, so nothing breaks for the person reading it. One-time setup in Settings. Then the runes follow you into every app.

RUNE PAD

A composer for inscriptions meant to be kept. Write vertically, read the line back in Latin, and carry it out as a transparent PNG, a plaque, or plain text. Real glyphs only survive as pictures, so that is how Rune Pad sends them.

TWO THEMES

Ritual and Arcadia. One curtain-rise between them, and nothing else that moves without a reason.

WHAT IT DOES NOT DO

There is no networking code in either target. No accounts, no analytics, no tracking, no data collection of any kind. Full Access is requested for one reason: iOS will not deliver haptic feedback from a keyboard extension without it. Leave Full Access off and everything except haptics behaves identically. The source is public, so this is checkable rather than promised.

ACCESSIBILITY

Full VoiceOver coverage, including the parts that are a game. Reduce Motion has a real alternative everywhere rather than a feature switched off. Dynamic Type scales through the accessibility sizes, and the alphabet chart reflows to two columns rather than truncating.

Ten things are hidden in the app. Finding all of them unlocks one more.

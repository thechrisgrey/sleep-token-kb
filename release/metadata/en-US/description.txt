The ritual alphabet, on every key.

Type the way you always have: the runes stay on the keycaps and ordinary English arrives on the other end, so nothing breaks for whoever is reading it.

Made by a fan, for fans. This is fan art — not an official app, and not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Sleep Token, their label, or their management.

THE KEYBOARD

A system keyboard whose keycaps wear the runes. One-time setup in Settings. Then the runes follow you into every app.

RUNE PAD

A composer for inscriptions meant to be kept. Write vertically, read the line back in Latin, and carry it out as a transparent PNG, a plaque, or plain text. Real glyphs only survive as pictures, so that is how Rune Pad sends them.

TWO THEMES

Obsidian and gold, or black stone and rose. One curtain-rise between them, and nothing else that moves without a reason.

WHAT IT DOES NOT DO

There is no networking code in either target. No accounts, no analytics, no tracking, no data collection of any kind. Nothing of the band's is bundled either: no artwork, no photography, no audio, and the one song reference opens Apple Music rather than playing anything itself.

Full Access is requested for one reason: iOS will not deliver haptic feedback from a keyboard extension without it. Leave Full Access off and everything except haptics behaves identically. The source is public, so this is checkable rather than promised.

ACCESSIBILITY

Full VoiceOver coverage, including the parts that are a game. Reduce Motion has a real alternative everywhere rather than a feature switched off. Dynamic Type scales through the accessibility sizes, and the alphabet chart reflows to two columns rather than truncating.
